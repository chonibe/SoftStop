SoftStop is a tiny open-source gate. Before email, SMS, modals, discounts, or AI nudges escalate, they call check/record. Deterministic cooldowns and caps across systems — not another messaging platform, not an agent firewall.

Your agents are individually smart. Collectively, they can still overwhelm a human — sales follows up, support escalates, retention discounts, marketing sends urgency. SoftStop gives every system the same answer before it increases pressure: is this action allowed right now?

Software knows how to push. SoftStop makes it stop when it should.
